# Profiles and personal data

## Your work profile

**Settings → Profile → Work profile** is yours to keep up in each workspace:

- your title and skills;
- the hours you work a week, and the focus factor (how much of that goes to planned work);
- your time zone and working hours;
- your quiet hours, when your phone stays silent and the inbox still fills.

Insights and Time read your hours a week, and notifications read your quiet hours. The focus factor
is kept for capacity planning.

An admin sets your **manager** and your **cost rate** under **People → Profile**. The cost rate (euro
an hour) feeds budgets and margins. Only admins and you see it. Colleagues see the rest of your
profile. The history says that a rate changed, never what it is.

## Your data

**Settings → Profile → Download my data** gives you everything Orqiva keeps about you, in every
workspace: one ZIP with a JSON file per kind of record. That covers:

- your account and your memberships and profiles;
- your sessions and tokens, by name only;
- the tasks you made or were given, your comments, messages and time;
- your inbox, your settings, and what you did in the activity.

The export finds every record that names you, so nothing added later is left out. Passwords, token
hashes, second-factor seeds and device keys are never in it.

An admin answers a request under the GDPR with **People → Profile → Download their data**. That ZIP
holds what this workspace keeps about the person, and is recorded in the activity.

## Erasing someone who left

Erasure comes after offboarding, which hands their open work and agents to someone else. An owner then
opens **People → Data** for the person, types their name, and erases.

What goes:

- their profile, their roles in projects and teams, and their inbox;
- their notification rules, settings and watched pages;
- what they wrote in direct messages;
- their membership.

What stays: the tasks, comments, documents and time they recorded belong to the team. They stay, under
**Erased person**.

When the person belongs to no other workspace, the account goes too. Their name and e-mail are
replaced, their password, sessions, tokens, devices, second factor and sign-in links are deleted, and
they can no longer sign in. When they belong to other workspaces, the account stays for those.

The erasure is recorded in the activity as "personal data erased". It cannot be undone.
